Privacy Policy for Daily Points

Last updated: 2026-11-03

Daily Points is published by Hexsoft Limited. This policy explains how Daily Points handles user and device data.

Information We Collect

Daily Points stores app data locally on your device, including task names, points, completion history, planned tasks, profile names, and app settings.

Daily Points does not require account registration and does not directly collect personal identifiers such as your name, phone number, or email address inside the app.

How We Use Data

Data is used only to provide app features such as task tracking, reminders, calendar planning, and CSV report sharing.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your app data with third parties for advertising or analytics.

If you choose to share exported CSV files, sharing is performed by you through your device's system share options.

Notifications

Daily Points requests notification permission to send reminders that you configure. You can disable notifications anytime from app settings.

Data Storage and Retention

Data is stored on your device and remains there until you delete it, clear app data, or uninstall the app.

Children's Privacy

Daily Points is not designed to collect personal data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a revised date.
